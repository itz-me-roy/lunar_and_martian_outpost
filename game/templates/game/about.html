What is simplified?

  • Game turns are time-compressed (about 2 Earth days per Moon turn).
  • "Storm shield" stands in for real shelters like water walls and moving into a shielded room.
  • The radiation "safety line" is a game limit, not an official medical limit.
  • The OSDR dataset is listed as background reading but is not yet used in the simulation.
