How to play

  1. Share your power. Turn the dials on the Air machine, Greenhouse, Storm shield and Science lab.
  2. No free choices. Power spent on science can't protect the crew from a storm.
  3. Listen to Earth (costs power) to get storm warnings. Flares give no warning at all!
  4. Keep the radiation meter under the line, and the air and food above zero.
